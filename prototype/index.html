Questions

Before you ask.

Is Sollu free?

Yes. Asking is free, and it always will be.

Is this legal advice?

No. Sollu explains rules in plain words. For a legal case, it connects you to free legal aid in your district.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Your saved answers stay on your phone.

What happens to my voice?

It's turned into text to answer you, then deleted. We never sell your data.

What if Sollu is wrong?

Tap "Report a wrong answer". A person checks it, and we fix the source.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. It's built for low-cost Android phones and slow networks.
