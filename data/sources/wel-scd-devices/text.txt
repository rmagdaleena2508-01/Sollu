Tricycles
×
Click here to view pdf version
1
Gist of the schemes
Tricycles are given to the deserving orthopaedically differently abled persons for their easy mobility.
2
Eligibility Criteria
The candidate should be affected with both lower limbs and their upper limbs are normal, resident of the district/area and their age should be more than 12 years.
3
Whether form of application is prescripbed
Yes. Applications are available with concerned District Differently Abled Welfare Officer.
4
Certificates to be furnished
National identity card for differently abled
5
Officer to whom the application is to be submitted
District Differently Abled Welfare Officer
