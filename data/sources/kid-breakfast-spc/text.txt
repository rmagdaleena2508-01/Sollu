Chief Minister’s Breakfast Scheme
Author
ramkumarb
Published
December 12, 2023
Overview
The Tamil Nadu Government is implementing Chief Minister’s Breakfast Scheme, a flagship programme covering all the children studying in Primary classes in government schools. The Scheme benefits about 17 lakh students from classes I to V. The scheme has been extended to 4154 Government Aided Schools in rural areas during 2024-2025.
The State Planning Commission has taken up an impact assessment study of the scheme in terms of improvements in school attendance, classroom engagement, and perception of parents and teachers about the scheme. The Study covers 100 schools both in rural and urban areas across the state. A total of 5274 primary school students are covered. Perception about the scheme among the teachers and parents of the students has been assessed.
The key findings of the study are
There is a significant increase in attendance rates across gender and locality.
There is a reduction in students arriving at school after 9 am across socio-demographic variables
There is a decrease in the proportion of students being hospitalized and experiencing ailments
The learning aptitude, active engagement in classroom learning and participation in extracurricular and sports activities of the children have shown remarkable improvement.
There is an increase in students following teachers’ instructions, paying attention and completing written work.
Around 80 per cent of students were able to recall the previous lessons.
There is an improvement in students’ handwriting, reading, and speaking abilities
The scheme was especially beneficial for rural families, particularly in families where parents left early for wok.
Mothers expressed satisfaction that their children are now eagerly consuming breakfast at school, relief from their concerns about their children skipping breakfast earlier.
