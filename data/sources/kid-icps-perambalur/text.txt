Administrative Structure
Objectives
To contribute to the improvements in the well being of children in difficult circumstances, as well as to the reduction of vulnerabilities to situations and actions that lead to abuse, neglect, exploitation, abandonment and separation of children.
List of on-goning Projects / Schemes with Target groups / List of documents which are required for availing the schemes.
Sponsorship:
Sponsorship is the provision of supplementary support to families to meet medical, nutritional, educational and other needs of their children with a view to improving their quality of life.
Target group:
Children, of the age of 0 to18 years.
Children of families are having Annual income below Rs.24,000/- in villages, Rs.30,000/- in Cities Rs.36,000/- in Metro Cities.
Children who are not availing any Financial Assistance from other Schemes of State / Central Government.
Children staying in child care institutions for more than six months continuously, who can be restored to their families.
Priority shall be given to:
Children of a single mother / widow
Children of Leprosy patients / HIV infected parent
Children whose parent / bread earner is in jail.
Financial Norms:
Rs.2000 per child per month for maximum 2 children per family.
Duration-Maximum three years or up to 18 years, whichever is earlier, other than exceptional circumstances.
List of documents required for availing the schemes:
Petition (with Passport size photo-1 No.)
Bonafide Certificate from Head master
Income Certificate
Bank Pass book copy Child
Aadhar Card Copy of Children & Parents
Ration Card Copy
Death Certificates of Parents (if Applicable)
Medical Records (if Applicable)
Other Document (if any)
Foster Care:
Foster Care means placement of a child, by the Child Welfare Committee for the purpose of alternate care in the domestic environment of a family, other than the child’s biological family, that has been selected, qualified, approved and supervised for providing such care.
Target group:
Children in the age group of 6-18 years who have been staying in child care institutions.
Children whose parents are terminally ill and have submitted a request to the Committee or the District Child Protection Unit for taking care of their child as they are unable to take care of their child.
whose parents are mentally ill and are unable to take care of the child;
whose one or both parents are in jail;
who are victims of physical, emotional or sexual abuse, natural disasters and domestic violence.
Financial Norms:
Rs.2000 per child per month for maximum 2 children per family.
Duration-Maximum three years or up to 18 years, whichever is earlier, other than exceptional circumstances.
Financial Support:
Wherever financial support is requested for the care of a child, after due approval, the foster carers / parents will be given financial support of Rs.2000 per month.
For more details please log on to: http://www.wcd.nic.in/acts/model-guidelines-foster-care-2016
