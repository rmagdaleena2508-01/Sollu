UYEGP
Scheme for mitigating the unemployment problems of
socially and economically weaker sections of the society
Applicants must be of age between 18-45 in general
and for special (Women/Minorities/ BC/MBC/SC/ST/Ex-servicemen/Transgenders/Differently
abled), age relaxation upto 55 years
Minimum 8 th Pass
Family income should be below Rs.8.00 lakh
Only Trading/Business projects with
maximum Project costs Rs.15.00 lakh respectively
Promoter’s contribution 10% for General and 5% for
Special Category
Loan assistance from Commercial Banks
Individual Based Capital Subsidy @ 25% of the
Project cost (Maximum Rs.3.75 lakh)
