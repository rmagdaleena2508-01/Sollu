Retrofitted Petrol Scooters
Read more
Retrofitted Petrol Scooters
×
Click here to view pdf version
1
Gist of the schemes
Retrofitted petrol scooters are provided for orthopaedically affected persons, whose both legs are affected. Students, employed and self employed differently abled persons are eligible
2
Eligibility Criteria
The age of the beneficiary should be more than 18 years. The candidate should be orthopaedically differently abled student / working in private organisations / self employed person and resident of the district / area.
3
Whether form of application is prescribed
Yes. Applications are available with concerned District Differently Abled Welfare Officer.
4
Certificates to be furnished
National identity card for differently abled.
5
Officer to whom the application is to be submitted
District Differently Abled Welfare Officer
6
Grievances if any to be addressed to
Technical Officer,

Gist of the schemes
In order to encourage the hearing impaired students to wear hearing aids to increase their speech capacity, Behind the ear hearing aids are provided.
