                                    FOREWORD

      “When hunger strikes, all ten virtues go away” (பசி வந்திடப் பத்தும்

பறந்துபபோகும்). Hence, to make Tamil Nadu a “Hunger free Vetri TamilNadu”,
the Tamil Nadu Government has resolved to extend all support to farmers who
continuously practice the noble agriculture profession to fulfill the food
requirement of all people. Accordingly, the Government of Tamil Nadu has
provided the Agriculture – Farmers Welfare Department with Vision schemes.

      Based on the Vision schemes, many schemes have been announced in
the Agriculture Budget 2026-27 and implemented for the benefit of the farming
community.

      Since bountiful yield can only be achieved if the soil fertility is preserved
without degradation, a special scheme called Tamil Nadu Soil Fertility Mission is
implemented. Further, excellent schemes such as providing quality seeds to
farmers; ensuring necessary irrigation facilities for agriculture; providing
required three phase electricity for agriculture; utilizing high-yielding and
hybrid varieties for good harvest; encouraging crop diversification to save
irrigation water; food and nutrition security; promoting organic and natural
farming    to     obtain   toxin-free    agricultural   produce;   integrated   crop
management; micro-irrigation; agroforestry for environmental protection and
income; agricultural mechanization to address labour shortage; initiatives for
enhancing farmers income and value addition as well as food processing for
agricultural produce are also formulated and being implemented.

      The details of various agricultural schemes to be implemented through
the Agriculture - Farmers Welfare Department in the year 2026–27 have been
compiled into Citizen Charter, designed for easy understanding by everyone.
This charter has been uploaded on the government website and made available
for public use.

      I proudly state that the Citizen Charter for the year 2026–27 will serve
as a helpful guide for farmers and the general public to know the details of
available schemes and benefit from them.


                                                         R. Vinoth

         6. TAMIL NADU AGRICULTURAL UNIVERSITY

       Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) is a globally recognized
institution delivering world-class agricultural education in agriculture and
allied subjects since 1971. Through offering 15 undergraduate, 34
postgraduate, and 29 doctoral programs, TNAU has awarded over 45,000
graduates including 30,230 UG, 11,397 PG, and 3,504 Ph.D. TNAU carries
research and innovation in frontier areas like Nanotechnology,
Biotechnology, Artificial Intelligence, Drone Technology, Remote sensing
Technology, Climatology, and Food processing and Value addition to find
solutions for field problems. Beyond academia, TNAU serves for framing
government agricultural policies for the benefit of the farming community.
Through its 15 KVKs, it offers support to farmers at grass root level to solve
field challenges by providing technical solutions, demonstrations and field
trainings. The Directorate of Agri-Business Development, empowers aspiring
youths to become entrepreneurs in agriculture and allied areas.
