Special Relief Assistance to Marine Fishermen Families during Fishing Lean Period
An amount of Rs.6,000/- is being given to Marine fishermen family as special relief assistance during the fishing lean period.
Eligibility Criteria to avail the assistance
a . The beneficiary should be a member of Fishermen Cooperative Society / Tamil Nadu Fishermen Welfare board and engaged in full-time fishing / allied fishing activities.
b. The beneficiary should possess a valid ration card.
c. Fishermen / Fishermen family members having regular employment or indulging in any other income-generating activity shall not qualify under this scheme.
Documents to be enclosed by the applicant
1. Application form
2. Copy of the family ration card
3. Copy of Fishermen Bio metric identity card
4. Proof for membership in Fishermen Cooperative Society / Copy of the Fishermen Welfare Board membership card
5. Copy of the photograph affixed in the first page of bank passbook
6. Copy of Aadhaar card
7. Copy of enrolment through the e-seva center
