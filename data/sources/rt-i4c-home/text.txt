Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre
Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C) was established by MHA, in New Delhi to provide a framework and eco-system
for Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) for dealing with Cybercrime in a coordinated and comprehensive manner.
I4C is envisaged to act as the nodal point to curb Cybercrime in the country.

Highlights
I4C Conducts Exposure Visit for 135 IPS Officer Trainees
(27 October 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, hosted an exposure visit for 135 IPS officer trainees to familiarise them with India's cybercrime prevention, detection and response framework. The programme commenced with an inaugural address by Shri Rajesh Kumar, CEO, I4C, followed by an address by Ms. Aishwarya Dongre, IPS, Deputy Director, OCWC. The sessions provided insights into I4C's functioning and the roles of its key units, including CFMC, JCCT, OCWC, NCTAU and Data Analytics teams. The initiative aimed to enhance institutional understanding, strengthen inter-agency coordination and build capacity for effective cybercrime prevention and response.
I4C Conducts Cybercrime Awareness Session at University Grants Commission (UGC)
(27 October 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, conducted a Cybercrime Awareness Session at the University Grants Commission (UGC) as part of Cyber Security Awareness Month. The session was led by Shri Nishant Kumar, Director, I4C, in hybrid mode, with online participation from around 1,000 colleges and universities across India. The programme focused on emerging modus operandi of cybercriminals, essential cyber safety practices, and the role of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) in promoting cybercrime awareness. The initiative aimed to strengthen cybersecurity awareness across educational institutions and contribute towards building a Cybercrime-Resilient India.
I4C Conducts Cyber Safety Awareness Session at Ministry of Culture
(27 October 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, conducted a Cyber Safety Awareness Session at the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, as part of Cyber Security Awareness Month. The session was led by Mrs. Nisha Pandey, Ecosystem Community Professional Developer, I4C, and Ms. Drishti Gupta, PR Strategist, I4C. The programme focused on emerging cyber threats, phishing, common online scams, key red flags, and safe digital practices. The initiative aimed to strengthen cyber awareness among Ministry personnel and promote cyber hygiene and responsible digital behaviour in the workplace.
I4C Participates in Child Online Safety Panel Discussion at c0c0n 2026
(10 October 2026)
Ms. Aishwarya Dongre, IPS, Deputy Director, Online Cybercrime Prevention Unit for Women & Children, Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, participated in the Protecting Tomorrow panel discussion at c0c0n 2026 in Kochi. The discussion focused on the opportunities and challenges associated with direct reporting of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) to India by technology platforms, including Meta and Google. The session highlighted the importance of timely reporting, effective response mechanisms and stronger coordination between law enforcement agencies, technology platforms and other stakeholders to combat online child sexual exploitation and strengthen child safety in the digital ecosystem.
S4C Connect – Workshop on Cryptocurrency and the Dark Web
(07 October 2026)
Strengthening India’s cybercrime response through stronger Centre–State coordination.
I4C, Ministry of Home Affairs, organised S4C Connect – Workshop on Cryptocurrency and Darkweb at Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi, bringing together senior leadership from MHA, I4C, State/UT Police and industry experts.
The programme was graced by Shri Govind Mohan, Union Home Secretary, Shri Rajesh Kumar, CEO, I4C, along with senior State cybercrime leadership.
I4C Conducts Exposure Visit and Capacity Building Session for BSF
(21 July 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, conducted an Exposure Visit and Capacity Building Session for Border Security Force (BSF) personnel to strengthen institutional preparedness against cybercrime. The programme provided participants with insights into India's cybercrime response ecosystem, key initiatives undertaken by I4C, and the evolving cyber threat landscape. It also highlighted collaborative approaches to cybercrime prevention, investigation, and capacity building across agencies. The initiative aimed to enhance knowledge, promote inter-agency coordination, and reinforce collective efforts towards building a cyber-resilient ecosystem through continuous learning, collaboration, and institutional capacity building.
I4C and University of Delhi Sign MoU for Cyber Safety and Capacity Building
(10 July 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, and University of Delhi signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to strengthen collaboration in cybercrime prevention, cyber awareness, and capacity building. The partnership aims to promote cyber hygiene through awareness programmes, workshops, seminars, hackathons, internships, cyber volunteer engagement, and academic initiatives. It will also facilitate research, knowledge sharing, and promotion of cybersecurity education, fostering stronger collaboration between academia and government to build a secure, resilient, and digitally responsible society.
I4C Conducts Cyber Crime Awareness Session with National Commission for Women
(07 July 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, in collaboration with the National Commission for Women (NCW), conducted a Cyber Crime Awareness and Capacity Building Session to promote cyber safety and strengthen digital resilience. The session focused on emerging cyber threats targeting women, safe digital practices, cyber fraud reporting mechanisms, and the importance of timely intervention in cybercrime cases. The initiative aimed to equip participants with practical knowledge and reinforce cyber awareness through continued collaboration and capacity-building efforts.
I4C and RBIH Sign MoU to Strengthen Fight Against Cyber Financial Frauds
(12 May 2026)
The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, and the Reserve Bank Innovation Hub (RBIH) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to strengthen collaboration in tackling cyber-enabled financial frauds and curbing mule accounts. The partnership will focus on fraud-risk intelligence sharing, analytical support, and operational coordination to enhance fraud detection and prevention. Under the initiative, I4C's Suspect Registry datasets will support AI-driven fraud detection systems, including MuleHunter.ai™, enabling early identification of suspicious financial activities and reinforcing a secure digital financial ecosystem.

Azadi Cyber Crime Se
(31st August 2025)
On the occasion of India’s 79th Independence Day, Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs, in collaboration with Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, and Ghaziabad Police, organised “Azadi Cyber Crime Se” – the biggest youth-led awareness movement across Delhi-NCR. This initiative witnessed 79 powerful street plays on a single day 31st August 2025, performed by Schools, Colleges & Theatre Societies in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, and Ghaziabad, uniting voices against cybercrime awareness. The event was a grand success as it spread awareness among a large number of students, teachers, parents and citizens in general, through the street plays at 79 different locations.
One Day Conference on "Cybercrime Awareness and Public Outreach" with Stakeholders
(27th June 2025)
A One-Day Conference on Cybercrime Awareness and Public Outreach was held on 27th June 2025, at the Indian Institute of Public Administration (IIPA), Delhi. The conference brought together key stakeholders, including representatives from banks, fintech companies, technology firms, and educational institutions such as NCERT, UGC, and various education boards (CBSE and State Boards). The primary objective was to develop a “Action Plan for Cybercrime Awareness”, aiming to align and streamline the efforts of all stakeholders to ensure a more coordinated, impactful, and far-reaching public awareness strategy across the country.
